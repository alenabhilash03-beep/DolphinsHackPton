# 5C.2 review — accounts, experiment sharing and hosted deployment

## Delivery

**Accounts (Google via Supabase Auth).** The browser signs in with Google through Supabase. The Next.js `/api/*` routes forward the user's own token, and FastAPI asks Supabase who it belongs to (cached for two minutes). `AUTH_MODE=off`, the default, keeps the local single-user demo unchanged.

| Signed out | Signed in |
| --- | --- |
| Saved demo, slider simulations, structures, drug assessment | Research agent, follow-up experiments, preparations, history, sharing, community feed |

**Per-user workspaces.** Result IDs are hashes of the configuration, and each result accepts one follow-up. On a shared server two users asking the same question would therefore have collided on results, history and the follow-up slot. Each signed-in user now gets separate preparation, result-tree, history and job stores (`apps/api/app/workspaces.py`). A shared semaphore bounds total concurrent agent runs.

**Share your experiment.** The panel under the workspace history publishes an immutable snapshot (`apps/api/app/research/sharing.py`) containing:

- the path of steps from the original question to the shared result (S1, S2, …), with changes, solver outcomes, verdicts, papers and structure;
- the shared result's curves and its cited answer;
- a deterministic **log summary**;
- an **AI summary** (NVIDIA Nemotron). Every statement must cite existing steps, every number must appear in a cited step, and clinical, safety or dosing claims are rejected. The model gets one corrective retry with the validator's errors; invalid statements are dropped, and if none survive the page says so and shows the log summary.

**Community.** `/community` is the feed of all shares, visible to signed-in users; `/shared/<id>` is the read-only page. Step chips in the AI summary jump to the cited log entries. Only the owner can remove a share. Owners are shown by Google display name and avatar; email addresses are never stored.

**Deployment configuration.** `render.yaml` (Render Blueprint: Starter plan, 1 GB disk at `/var/data`, secrets entered in the dashboard), Vercel settings and the step-by-step [deployment guide](../DEPLOYMENT.md).

### Fixed during 5C.2

- **Shared result missing from a long history.** History windows hold 80 steps, and repeated slider releases can exceed that, so the shared result could fall outside the window (live 404). Shares now record the lineage, the path from the original question to the shared result, which is also the clearer story for readers.
- When the lineage has no agent-research step, the question falls back to the hypothesis shown in the workspace instead of the word "Simulation".

## Plan assessment

**Keep the plan,** with one scope note. The shared AI summary currently summarizes solver outcomes and recorded findings; it does not re-read papers. Experiments live in SQLite on the Render disk, which is fine for a hackathon-scale service; a managed database would be the next step for real multi-instance hosting.
