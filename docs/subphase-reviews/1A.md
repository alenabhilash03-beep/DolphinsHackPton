# Subphase 1A review — units, administration, and pharmacokinetics

## Delivered

- `apps/api/app/simulation/units.py` converts dose, concentration, time, clearance, and kinetic rates into the canonical units defined in `docs/model.md`. Conversions reject nonfinite, negative, or physically invalid inputs; overflow cannot silently enter a result.
- `apps/api/app/simulation/pk.py` resolves linked volume, clearance, and effective half-life edits with one explicitly changed field. At fixed volume, half-life edits derive clearance; clearance edits derive half-life. A volume edit holds clearance fixed.
- The pure PK compiler accepts validated finite IV infusion events, with a separately labeled hypothetical bolus requiring explicit opt-in. It rejects overlapping, unsorted, and post-horizon events. The ordinary profile therefore cannot accidentally use a bolus through this interface.
- It splits the time domain at event starts and ends and propagates the one-compartment amount exactly under each constant input rate. Its `expm1` form remains stable for small elimination steps. A partial infusion at the horizon delivers only its elapsed fraction. A dose beginning exactly at the horizon contributes nothing within the observation window.
- `PKTrajectory` supplies exact amount and central concentration at requested times, along with administration boundaries for later binding integration and graph sampling. A bolus returns its post-jump amount at the event time; concentration is never interpolated across that jump.

The model remains the **conditional exploratory reduction** documented in `docs/model.md`. The implementation does not make the selected population model components or central concentration into measured free target-site exposure. The analytical PK implementation is independent of LangChain and FastAPI.
