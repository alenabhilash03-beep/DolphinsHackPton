# 8C.2 review — Agent-to-viewer handoff and structural interpretation

## Delivered

- **Agent inspection in research turns.** `inspect_verified_structure` runs before the answer is written and records either "no verified structure" or the structure and its five closest drug–target contacts. `measure_structure_residue` lets the agent request up to two specific residues. Results are stored on `AgentTurn.structure` with measurement IDs.
- **Coordinate data in the answer.** The answer can include geometry-only structural statements, each citing measurement IDs. Residue numbers and distances must be those of the cited measurements. Statements that infer hydrogen bonds, energy, affinity, kinetics, mutation effects or occupancy are omitted. The verdict cannot rest on structure.
- **Honest scope.** A server-written note says what coordinates cannot decide, or that no verified structure exists and that research and simulation are unaffected.
- **Viewer handoff.** Measurement cards in the answer and under the structure tool event in the investigation log open the same residue pair in the molecular viewer. The viewer recomputes the distance from its own coordinates. Repeated requests work; manual inspection and the tour are unchanged.
- **Ledger.** A new `structure` stage; events carry the PDB ID, coordinate hash, pair count and measurement IDs only.
- **Synthesis robustness.** After one corrective retry, invalid list items are dropped whole and the validated remainder is kept, with a note saying how many were omitted. Misplaced answer references and a run-on limitations string are normalized.

## Observations

- Inspection adds one model round trip to every research turn, including drugs with no structure. The tool itself takes milliseconds.
- Parameter edits do not inspect structure, so after an edit in words the answer panel has no structural section. The viewer and manual inspection remain.
- The inference filter is a word list. It blocks the named inferences; it cannot judge every possible over-reading of a distance.
- Salvage changes what "grounded" can mean: an answer may now be shown with some proposed items omitted. The omission count is shown under the answer.
